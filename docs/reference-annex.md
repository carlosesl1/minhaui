# Visual Reference Annex

## Purpose and legal boundary

These eight screenshots are preserved as documentation-only evidence for interaction analysis. They are reference-only, are not runtime assets, are not shipped in application packages, and must never be copied into product UI. The implementation must use original Windows-native composition, copy, icons, and artwork. No Apple, macOS, SF Symbols, SF Pro, MyDockFinder, or other third-party branded assets are permitted. The screenshots may inform behavior, hierarchy, density, and timing only.

The design translation is **Obsidian Glass**: Windows-native materials and Segoe UI Variable, with restrained dock and menu interaction cues. Similarity is limited to familiar spatial behavior; visual expression and assets remain original.

## Shared fidelity rules

- Translate interaction principles, not pixels. The reference images are not a pixel-match target.
- Use Windows APIs and Windows terminology. When the app cannot verify a capability, hide it or mark it unavailable with a reason.
- Popovers share one owner at a time, anchor to the invoking item, stay inside the work area, flip when space is constrained, and close through Escape/outside click while restoring focus.
- All critical states are represented in code UI: default, hover, pressed, selected/on, focus-visible, disabled/unavailable, loading, empty, offline, error, attention, and drag target when applicable.
- Appearance degrades according to the performance ladder in `DESIGN.md`; behavior and readability do not degrade.
